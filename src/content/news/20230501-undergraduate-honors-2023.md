Three undergraduate students from Emory NLP earned their degrees with honors:

#### [Camila Calviño](/people/camila-calvino/)

- Highest Honor in Quantitative Theory and Methods
- Thesis: [The Emora College Companion: A Socialbot for College Students](/papers/theses/2023-honors-camila-calvino/)

#### [Chen Gong](/people/chen-gong/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Evaluating Speaker Diarization in Transcripts: A Text-based Approach with the TDER Metric and the TranscribeView System](/papers/theses/2023-honors-chen-gong/)

#### [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Analyzing Competitive Programming Competitions to Develop Effective Training Methods for Improving Problem-Solving Skills in Students](/papers/theses/2023-honors-alexandru-rudi/)
