Dr. [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) gave an invited talk at the NSF Future Directions Workshop on the Automatic Evaluation of Open-Domain Dialog (AED). Thanks to Dr. [Maxine Eskenazi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maxine-eskenazi-8a61b1/), who is a Principal Systems Scientist at Carnegie Mellon University, for organizing this workshop.
