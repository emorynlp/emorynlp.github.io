[SouthNLP 2024](https://southnlp.github.io/southnlp2024/), hosted by Emory University (General Chair: [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/)), proved to be a fruitful and vibrant symposium. With 150+ attendees representing 20+ schools, the event was a resounding success. Heartfelt gratitude goes out to all who participated, and special appreciation is extended to our esteemed keynote speakers, Dr. [Kathleen McKeown](http://www.cs.columbia.edu/~kathy/) from Columbia University and Dr. [Mohit Bansal](https://www.cs.unc.edu/~mbansal/) from the University of North Carolina.

We've shared [photos](https://photos.app.goo.gl/KtMH3jo1Swo3kEy87) from the event, so please take a moment to enjoy them!
