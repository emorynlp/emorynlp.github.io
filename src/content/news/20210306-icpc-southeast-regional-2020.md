The **M||E** team from Emory University won the bronze medal at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2020/) for Division 1. The contest was held virtually due to the pandemic. It is the second consecutive year that the Emory team has won the bronze medal for Division 1, the most comparative league. Here is a list of the contestants:

- [Samir Chowdhury](https://www.emorynlp.org/bachelors/samir-chowdhury) (Sophomore)
- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Sophomore)
- Katie Tyrone (Junior)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
