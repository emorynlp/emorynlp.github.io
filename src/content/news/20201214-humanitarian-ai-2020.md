[Sarah Finch](/people/sarah-finch/), [James Finch](/people/james-finch/), and [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) had an interview with [Humanitarian AI Today](https://soundcloud.com/humanitarian-ai-today/jinho-choi-sarah-fillwock-james-finch) about their winning strategy of the [Alexa Prize Socialbot Grand Challenge 3](https://www.emorynlp.org/projects/alexa-prize-3). Sarah Finch and James Finch were the two team leads of the Emora team who won the 1st-place at this competition.
