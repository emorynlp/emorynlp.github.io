The M||E team from Emory University qualified for the [ICPC North America Championship 2023](https://nac.icpc.global/history/year2023/) for the fourth consecutive time. The championship was held at the [University of Central Florida](https://www.ucf.edu/). Here is a list of the team members:

- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Senior)
- Simon Bian (Junior)
- Harry He (Junior)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
- Michelangelo Grigni (Co-Coach)
