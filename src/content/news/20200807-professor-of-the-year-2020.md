Dr. [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) received the "Professor of the Year" Award from the Computer Science department at Emory University. Here is a list of recipients for all awards this year:

- Educator of the Year: [Nosayba El-Sayed](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nosayba/) (Lecturer)
- Researcher of the Year: [Joyce Ho](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joyce-ho-05a12312/) (Assistant Professor)
- Citizen of the Year: [Dorian Arnold](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drdorianarnold/) (Associate Professor)
- Professor of the Year: [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Assistant Professor)
