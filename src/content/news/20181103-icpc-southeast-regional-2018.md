The **M||E** team from Emory University won the 1st-place at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2018/) for Division 2. The contest was held at [Kennesaw State University](https://www.kennesaw.edu/). It is the second consecutive year that the Emory team has won this contest for Division 2. Here is a list of the contestants:

- [Jose Coves](/people/jose-coves/) (Senior)
- [Chenxi Xu](/people/chenxi-xu/) (Junior)
- [Liyan Xu](/people/liyan-xu/) (MS student)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
