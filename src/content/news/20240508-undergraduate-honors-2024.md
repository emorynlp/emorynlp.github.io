Four undergraduate students from Emory NLP earned their degrees with honors:

#### [Jacob Choi](/people/jacob-choi/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [When Large Language Models Meet Religious Text](/papers/theses/2024-honors-jacob-choi/)

#### [Ellie Paek](/people/ellie-paek/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Emora Assistant Bot: Revolutionizing Task Automation and Chatbot Evaluation](/papers/theses/2024-honors-ellie-paek/)

#### [Peilin Wu](/people/peilin-wu/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Audio: Advancing Speaker Diarization with Text-based Methodologies and Comprehensive Evaluation](/papers/theses/2024-honors-peilin-wu/)

#### [Zinc Zhao](/people/boxin-zhao/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Impact of Data Analysis in Nascent NLP Tasks](/papers/theses/2024-honors-boxin-zhao/)
