Five undergraduate students from Emory NLP earned their degrees with honors:

#### [Jose Coves](/people/jose-coves/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [End-to-end Plural Coreference Resolution on TV Show Transcripts](/papers/theses/2019-honors-jose-coves/)

#### [Shen Gao](/people/shen-gao/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Cloud-based Active Learning System for Question Answering on Multiparty Dialogue](/papers/theses/2019-honors-shen-gao/)

#### [Xinyi Jiang](/people/xinyi-jiang/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Incremental Sense Weight Training for Contextualized Word Embedding Interpretation](/papers/theses/2019-honors-xinyi-jiang/)

#### [Mengmei Li](/people/mengmei-li/)

- Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Design of Meta-semantic Analysis for Automatic Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease](/papers/theses/2019-honors-mengmei-li/)

#### [Zhengzhe Yang](/people/zhengzhe-yang/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [FriendsQA: Open-Domain Question Answering Dataset on TV Show Transcripts](/papers/theses/2019-honors-zhengzhe-yang/)
