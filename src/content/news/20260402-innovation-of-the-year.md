Emory NLP’s [**Tinker Tales**](https://news.emory.edu/stories/2026/04/er_tinker_tales_literacy_app_17-04-2026/story.html)—an interactive storytelling platform that teaches AI literacy to young learners through play—was named **Innovation of the Year** at the [Office of Technology Transfer’s 20th Annual Celebration of Technology and Innovation](https://news.emory.edu/stories/2026/04/er_technology_innovations_awards_15-04-2026/story.html). Emory’s announcement credits **Jinho Choi**, **Ikseon Choi**, and **Nayoung Choi** with the underlying research. The ceremony at the Emory Conference Center Hotel’s Silverbell Pavilion recognized outstanding research and innovations across the university.
