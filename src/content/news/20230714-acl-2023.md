Three papers from Emory NLP are presented at the Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) 2023:

1. [Don’t Forget Your ABC’s: Evaluating the State-of-the-Art in Chat-Oriented Dialogue Systems](/papers/2023-acl-finch/). Sarah Finch, James Finch, Jinho D. Choi.
2. [Unleashing the True Potential of Sequence-to-Sequence Models for Sequence Tagging and Structure Parsing](/papers/2023-tacl-he/), Han He, Jinho D. Choi
3. [Towards Open-World Product Attribute Mining: A Lightly-Supervised Approach](/papers/2023-acl-xu/). Liyan Xu, Chenwei Zhang, Xian Li, Jingbo Shang, Jinho D. Choi

---

We had a Colorado & Emory Lunch Gathering with 24 people, including Dr. [Martha Palmer](https://www.colorado.edu/linguistics/martha-palmer) (CU), Dr. [James Martin](https://home.cs.colorado.edu/~martin/) (CU), Dr. [Alexis Palmer](https://www.colorado.edu/linguistics/alexis-palmer) (CU), Dr. [Dan Jurfsky](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/) (Stanford), Dr. [Dmitriy Dligach](https://www.dmitriydligach.com) (Loyola), Dr. [Sameer Pradhan](https://cemantix.org) (LDC), Dr. [Fei Liu](https://www.cs.emory.edu/~fliu40/) (Emory), and Dr. [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Emory).
