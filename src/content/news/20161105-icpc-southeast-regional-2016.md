The **Null Contest** team from Emory University won the 3rd-place at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2016/) for Division 2. The contest was held at [Kennesaw State University](https://www.kennesaw.edu/). Here is a list of the contestants:

- [Henry Chen](/people/henry-chen/) (Senior)
- Arnold Lin (Senior)
- [Ethan Zhou](/people/ethan-zhou/) (Junior)
- Denis Savenkov (Coach)
