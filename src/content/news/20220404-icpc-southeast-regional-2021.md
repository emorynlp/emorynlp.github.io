The **M||E** (pronounced, ɛm·ɔr·ee) team from Emory University won the silver medal at the [International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2021/) for Division 1. The contest was held at Mercer University. It is the third consecutive year that the Emory team has been among the top-3 for Division 1, the most comparative league. The contestants are:

- [Samir Chowdhury](https://www.emorynlp.org/bachelors/samir-chowdhury) (Junior)
- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Junior)
- Carol Tang (Senior)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
