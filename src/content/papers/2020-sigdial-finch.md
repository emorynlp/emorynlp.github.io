abstract: >-
  As conversational AI-based dialogue management has increasingly become a trending topic, the
  need for a standardized and reliable evaluation procedure grows even more pressing. The
  current state of affairs suggests various evaluation protocols to assess chat-oriented
  dialogue management systems, rendering it difficult to conduct fair comparative studies
  across different approaches and gain an insightful understanding of their values. To foster
  this research, a more robust evaluation protocol must be set in place. This paper presents a
  comprehensive synthesis of both automated and human evaluation methods on dialogue systems,
  identifying their shortcomings while accumulating evidence towards the most effective
  evaluation dimensions. A total of 20 papers from the last two years are surveyed to analyze
  three types of evaluation protocols: automated, static, and interactive. Finally, the
  evaluation dimensions used in these papers are compared against our expert evaluation on the
  system-user dialogue data collected from the Alexa Prize 2020.
