abstract: >-
  Importance: Timely access to human expertise for affordable and efficient triage of
  ophthalmic conditions is inconsistent. With recent advancements in publicly available
  artificial intelligence (AI) chatbots, individuals may turn to these tools for triage of
  ophthalmic complaints. Validation studies are necessary to evaluate the performance of AI
  chatbots as triage tools and inform the public regarding their safety.
