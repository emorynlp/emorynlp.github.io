abstract: >-
  Introduction: Advances in natural language processing, speech recognition and machine
  learning allow the exploration of linguistic and acoustic changes previously difficult to
  measure. We developed processes for deriving lexical-semantic and acoustic measures as
  Alzheimer’s disease (AD) digital voice biomarkers, and evaluated the clinical sensitivity of
  these derived measures against neurocognitive, neuroimaging, and CSF AD biomarker data.
