Dr. **Hyopil Shin** is a Professor of Linguistics at Seoul National University, where he directs the [Computational Linguistics Lab](http://knlp.snu.ac.kr) and the [Ambient NLP Lab](https://sites.google.com/snu.ac.kr/gsds-nlp/). Hyopil spent a year as a visiting scholar at Emory NLP.
