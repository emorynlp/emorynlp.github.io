Dr. [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) gave a talk at [TEDxEmory 2022](https://www.tedxemory.org/conference2022). The theme of this year's conference was "Exigent: If Not Now, When?", and the title of Dr. Choi's talk was "How should we prepare for the ear of AI?". Thanks to [Tia Bakhaya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tia-bakhaya-6057b1193/) for inviting Dr. Choi and [Sahana Mathiarasan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sahanamathi/) for organizing the event.
