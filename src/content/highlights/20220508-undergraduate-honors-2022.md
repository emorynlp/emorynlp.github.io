Six undergraduate students from Emory NLP earned their degrees with honors:

#### [Angela Cao](/people/angela-cao/)

- Highest Honor in Linguistics
- Thesis: [An Analysis of Causal Language Constructions in Diverse Discourse Data](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-angela-cao/)

#### [Yingying Chen](/people/yingying-chen/)

- Highest Honor in Quantitative Theory and Methods
- Thesis: [Analysis of Temporal Relations in Various Types of Text Data](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-yingying-chen/)

#### [Sophy Huang](/people/xiaoyuan-huang/)

- Highest Honor in Mathematics
- Thesis: [Unsupervised, Context-Aware Emotion Classification of College-Related Reddit](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-xiaoyuan-huang/)

#### [Mack Hutsell](/people/william-hutsell/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Automatic Generation of Multi-turn Dialogues from Reddit](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-william-hutsell/)

#### [Daniil Huryn](/people/daniil-huryn/)

- Highest Honor in Computer Science
- Thesis: [Framework for Automatic Generation of Large-scale Dialogue Data from Online Forums](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-daniil-huryn/)

#### [Jessica Ji](/people/yuxin-ji/)

- Highest Honor in Quantitative Theory and Methods
- Thesis: [Enriching and Evaluating Meaning Representations](/publications/theses/honors-thesis-2022-yuxin-ji/)
