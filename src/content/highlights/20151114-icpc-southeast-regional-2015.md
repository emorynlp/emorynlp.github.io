The **Null Contest** team from Emory University won the 2nd-place at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2015/) for Division 2. The contest was held at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/). Here is a list of the contestants:

- Benjamin Bolte (Senior)
- [Henry Chen](/people/henry-chen/) (Junior)
- [Johnny Tan](/people/johnny-tan/) (Senior)
- Denis Savenkov (Coach)
