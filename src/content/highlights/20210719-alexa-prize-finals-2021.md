The Emora team made it to the Finals of the [Alexa Prize Socialbot Grand Challenge 4](https://www.emorynlp.org/projects/alexa-prize-4). It is the second consecutive year that the Emora team has made to the Finals of this competition. The Finals will take place on July 26th and the winner will be announced in August. Here is a list of the team members:
