Dr. [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) and [Gary Lai](/people/gary-lai/) gave a chalk talk, [How Emory University Built an NLP Platform Using Apache MXNet and GluonNLP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QNDHtidI_SqyvTZv5-BHrGCVEQx5pHRS/view?usp=sharing) at the [AWS re:Invent 2018](https://reinvent.awsevents.com/) and presented the cloud-based NLP research platform called [ELIT](https://www.emorynlp.org/projects/elit-nlp-toolkit) (Emory Language and Information Toolkit).
