The Emora team won the [Alexa Prize Socialbot Grand Challenge 3](https://www.amazon.science/latest-news/amazon-announces-2020-alexa-prize-winner-emory-university). Our final average rating was 3.81 compared to 3.17 and 3.14 achieved by the 2nd and 3rd-place winners, Stanford University and Czech Technical University, respectively.
