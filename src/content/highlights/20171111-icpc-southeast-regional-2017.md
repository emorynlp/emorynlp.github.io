The **M||E** team from Emory University won the 1st-place at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2017/) for Division 2. The contest was held at [Kennesaw State University](https://www.kennesaw.edu/). It is the first time that the Emory team wins this contest for Division 2. Here is a list of the contestants:

- Benjamin Bolte (MS student)
- [Jose Coves](/people/jose-coves/) (Junior)
- Sergey Volokhin (PhD student)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
