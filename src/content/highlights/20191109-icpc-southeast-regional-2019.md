The **M||E** team from Emory University won the 3rd-place at the [ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2019/) for Division 1. The contest was held at [Kennesaw State University](https://www.kennesaw.edu/). It was the first time that the Emory team challenged Division 1, the most comparative league, and got a great result. Here is a list of the contestants:

- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Freshman)
- [Chenxi Xu](/people/chenxi-xu/) (Senior)
- Yusen Zhang (MS student)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
