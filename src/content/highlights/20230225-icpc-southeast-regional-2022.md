The **M||E** (pronounced, ɛm·ɔr·ee) team from Emory University won the gold medal for Augusta site and the bronze medal at the [International Collegiate Programming Contest: Southeast USA Region](http://seusa.vanb.org/ser2021/). It is the fourth consecutive year that the Emory team has been among the top-3 for Division 1, the most comparative league, in this region. The contestants and coaches are:

- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Senior)
- Simon Bian (Junior)
- Harry He (Junior)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
- Michelangelo Grigni (Co-Coach)
