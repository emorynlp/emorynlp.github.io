The **M||E** team from Emory University made it to the [ICPC North America Championship 2020](https://nac.icpc.global/history/year2020/) for the first time. The championship was held at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/). Here is a list of the team members:

- [Alexandru Rudi](/people/alexandru-rudi/) (Freshman)
- [Chenxi Xu](/people/chenxi-xu/) (Senior)
- Yusen Zhang (MS student)
- [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/) (Coach)
