Two papers from Emory NLP are presented at the Annual Meeting of the Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue (SIGDIAL) 2024:

1. [Transforming Slot Schema Induction with Generative Dialogue State Inference](/publications/2024-sigdial-finch/). James D. Finch, Boxin Zhao, Jinho D. Choi.
2. [Automating PTSD Diagnostics in Clinical Interviews: Leveraging Large Language Models for Trauma Assessments](/publications/2024-sigdial-tu/). Sichang Tu, Abigail Powers, Natalie Merrill, Negar Fani, Sierra Carter, Stephen Doogan, Jinho D. Choi.
