**Tinker Tales**, an interactive storytelling platform that teaches AI literacy to young learners through play, developed by [Jinho Choi](/people/jinho-choi/), Ikseon Choi, and [Nayoung Choi](/people/nayoung-choi/), received the **Innovation of the Year Award** from the Emory Office of Technology Transfer at the [20th Annual Celebration of Technology and Innovation](https://news.emory.edu/stories/2026/04/er_technology_innovations_awards_15-04-2026/story.html).
