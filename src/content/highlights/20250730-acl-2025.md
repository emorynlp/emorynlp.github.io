Three papers from Emory NLP are presented at the Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) 2025:

1. [Finding A Voice: Exploring the Potential of African American Dialect and Voice Generation for Chatbots](https://www.emorynlp.org/publications/acl-2025-finch-et-al). Sarah E. Finch, Ellie S. Paek, and Jinho D. Choi.
2. [D-GEN: Automatic Distractor Generation and Evaluation for Reliable Assessment of Generative Models](https://www.emorynlp.org/publications/acl-findings-2025-byun-and-choi). Grace Byun and Jinho D. Choi
3. [Beyond Linear Digital Reading: An LLM-Powered Concept Mapping Approach for Reducing Cognitive Load](https://www.emorynlp.org/publications/bea-2025-han-and-choi). Junzhi Han and Jinho D. Choi.
