abstract: 'Cuddles ANC is an AI-enabled plush companion designed to speak and listen naturally while remaining truly “plush.” The system pairs a low-power embedded MCU with a cloud speech pipeline (ASR → LLM → TTS) to deliver real-time, screen-free interaction via wake word and voice commands. Our work centers on three challenge areas. (1) Audio & real-time interaction: Latency arose not only from embedded audio handling but also from the server pipeline—especially TTS. We tested multiple TTS engines and adopted streaming synthesis, prompt/voice tuning, and connection reuse to reduce first-audio delay while preserving speech quality. (2) Natural conversation & HCI: To sustain engagement, we added lightweight memory (name/preferences), consistent character persona, routines/streaks, and parental controls (time caps, review, safety filters). (3) Integration & safety: We balanced comfort and reliability—maintaining the plush feel, managing thermals, and using magnetic wireless charging with appropriate safeguards. Future directions include deploying Cuddles as a research testbed (e.g., LENA-alternative studies), expanding to elder companionship and wellness check-ins, and progressively shifting toward on-device inference for lower latency and stronger privacy.'
