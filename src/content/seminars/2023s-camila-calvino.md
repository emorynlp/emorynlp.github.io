abstract: >-
  The Emora College Companion is a conversational socialbot designed by Emory
  University's Natural Language Processing Lab to assist undergraduate students in
  achieving their academic goals. While other chatbots in higher education provide
  basic information and reminders, the Emora College Companion engages in human-like
  interactions to personalize the student experience. With a misunderstanding rate
  of 5.05% and a poor response rate of 4.46%, version 8 of the College Companion
  is capable of over 40 personalized conversations across 13 topics, and has a total
  conversation time of approximately 56 minutes. This technology is the first
  socialbot catering to college undergraduate students and provides a unique approach
  to tackling day-to-day tasks and challenges.
