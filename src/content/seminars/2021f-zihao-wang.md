abstract: In this project, we plan to build an InterviewBot that interviews students who are non-native speakers. Such an InterviewBot is designed to conduct fluent conversations with interviewees covering main topics, such as basic information, academic interests, previous projects, future plans, etc. In this presentation, I will elaborate on the first phase of the project planning, including data annotation, topic segmentation, and information retrieval.
