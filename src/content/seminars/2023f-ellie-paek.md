abstract: In the fast-paced world of business and education, automating mundane administrative tasks has become imperative. This project develops the Emora Assistant Bot, which aims to offer an innovative solution to streamline tasks such as scheduling appointments, providing feedback, and facilitating communication. Emora achieves this by harnessing the power of an advanced State Transition Dialogue Manager (STDM) and functional assistance from large language models, including GPT-3.5. This collaboration between AI and cutting-edge technology holds the potential to expand chatbot understanding and reshape how we manage everyday business assignments and tasks.
