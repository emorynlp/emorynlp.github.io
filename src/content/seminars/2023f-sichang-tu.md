abstract: 'This project is a collaboration with the Grady Trauma Research Team from the School of Medicine at Emory University. Its goal is to augment support, assessment, and intervention for PTSD (Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder) by harnessing the capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) and conversational AI. In this presentation, I will detail the processing of interview datasets garnered from the Grady Trauma Project. This includes transcription, speaker diarization, alignment, and segmentation. Furthermore, I will introduce the model''s framework, which is bifurcated into two main components: the generation model and the assessment model. I will also share preliminary results for both components.'
