paperUrl: 'https://aclanthology.org/P16-3020/'
abstract: >-
  This paper introduces a new corpus, QA-It, for the classification of non-referential it. Our
  dataset is unique in a sense that it is annotated on question answer pairs collected from
  multiple genres, useful for developing advanced QA systems. Our annotation scheme makes
  clear distinctions between 4 types of it, providing guidelines for many erroneous cases.
  Several statistical models are built for the classification of it, showing encouraging
  results. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first time that such a corpus is created
  for question answering.
